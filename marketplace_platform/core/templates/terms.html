2. About the Platform

The Platform connects local food producers within a twenty-mile radius of Bristol city centre with consumers including individual customers, community groups, and independent restaurants. It is operated by the Bristol Regional Food Network ("the Network").

  • Accounts are non-transferable.
  • We reserve the right to suspend or terminate accounts that violate these terms.

4. Producer Obligations

  • Producers must ensure all product listings are accurate, including descriptions, allergen information, pricing, and availability.
  • Producers are responsible for the quality and safety of their products.
  • Producers must provide a minimum 48-hour lead time for order fulfilment.
  • Producers must maintain up-to-date stock levels to avoid unfulfillable orders.
  • Producers must comply with all applicable food safety and labelling legislation.

5. Customer Obligations

  • Customers must provide accurate delivery address and contact information.
  • Customers are responsible for checking allergen information before placing orders.
  • Orders must be placed in good faith and with genuine intent to purchase.

6. Payments & Commission

  • All payments are processed securely through the Platform's payment system.
  • The Network charges a 5% commission on all transactions.
  • Producers receive 95% of the order value, settled weekly.
  • Payment settlements include a transparent audit trail suitable for tax and accounting purposes.

7. Food Safety & Quality

  • Customers with allergies or dietary requirements must verify product suitability directly with producers.
  • The Network may remove listings that fail to meet food safety standards.

8. Liability
