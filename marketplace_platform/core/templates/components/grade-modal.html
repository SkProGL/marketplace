What each metric means

How confident the neural network is that the item is healthy and fit for sale. Scored 0–100%. This is the only metric that can trigger a rejection. If the model determines the item is damaged, over-ripe, or otherwise unfit, it is rejected outright - colour and proportion scores are recorded but carry no weight in the final decision.

Similarity of the item's colour profile to reference distributions for fresh produce, measured using Bhattacharyya histogram distance. Higher means the colour is more typical of healthy, market-ready produce.

How regular and well-formed the item's shape is, derived from the ratio of its area to its convex hull area (contour solidity). A perfectly solid shape scores 100%. Irregular or fragmented shapes score lower.
